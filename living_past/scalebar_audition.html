The human belongs in the key, not the world

A 1.8 m human silhouette next to the giants, drawn to a shared meter ruler, lets a viewer calibrate the whole poster at a glance — and it does so in the furniture, so no anachronistic human ever intrudes on the Cretaceous scene. Two layouts below.

Recommendation

A — the horizontal ruler strip. One shared metre scale with the human and the two titans on it turns "measured, not eyeballed" from a claim into something you can see, and it fits the facts band's horizontal rhythm. B (height stack) is a nice web-page module but on the poster it competes with the confidence key for the same corner.
