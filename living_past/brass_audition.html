Recommendation

Flat brass as the locked base for all small/repeated furniture — the 32 confidence dots, per-card rules, callout numerals, QR frames. At true print size, texture on these just becomes noise or a banding hazard, and flat brass is what keeps the field guide reading as crisp "engraved fine print."

Reserve a whisper of brushed sheen (option B) for the few LARGE brass moments only — the master-QR frame and the confidence-key plate in the facts band — where the surface is big enough to carry it. This is a Photoshop finishing touch at build time (layer style), not a template constant, so it costs nothing to defer.

Net lock for §4: brass = flat (#D8B57A highlight / #B98F4E base); optional subtle brushed layer-style allowed on elements ≥ ~1.5 cm only.
