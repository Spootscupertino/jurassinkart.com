## What we actually accomplished

**Locked all the design decisions (goals #1–10).** Each one got a real, rendered
audition (open the `*_audition.html` files to see them):
- **Fonts:** Cinzel for titles/numbers, EB Garamond for names/body, Optima for labels.
  You picked all three from a live side-by-side. Also set the full size ladder and the
  brass-label spacing.
- **Brass:** flat (no fake metal texture), except a subtle sheen allowed on big pieces.
- **Confidence badge:** one brass dot, filled/half/open = well-documented / reasonable / speculative.
- **Asteroid whisper:** IN — a faint cold point in the empty top-left sky.
- **Depth-zone lines:** thin brass hairlines that fade in from the right edge (so the
  water stays immersive, not diagram-y).
- **Type icons:** seven brass silhouettes (land animal, flyer, marine reptile, fish,
  invertebrate, plant, mammal), saved to `glyphs.svg`.
- **Scale key:** a metre ruler with a human + the biggest animal, so people grasp the
  scale. The human never appears inside the scene.

**Built the real tools (goals #11–15).**

  a CSS generator + a Photoshop template-skeleton script.
- Wrote `scale_calc.py` — turns "12–13 m" into exact pixels on the poster, and enlarges
  tiny things (ants, worms) honestly with their true size noted.
- Locked the 32-organism roster in `volume_v.json`. **Important catch:** a code check
  (`roster_audit.py`) found 7 animals in the old list that were millions of years out of
  place. You chose the "true 66-million-year-old community," so we swapped them for real
  end-Cretaceous animals. Now 0 errors, perfectly balanced 8/8/8/8.
- Built the QR redirect layer and the Astro web-page template that auto-builds a page for
  every organism (both staged, live site untouched).

**Prep + business (goals #17, 19, 20).**

- Designed the organism web page (rendered `organism_page.html`).
