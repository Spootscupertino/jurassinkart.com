# CR21 — *Champsosaurus* dossier
**Version:** 1.0.0-pilot · **Reviewed:** 2026-07-29 · **Review:** None

## Audit
Freshwater semi-aquatic choristodere, not marine and not a crocodilian. Genus-level naming is preferred; terminal Maastrichtian presence and Paleocene survival are supported.
