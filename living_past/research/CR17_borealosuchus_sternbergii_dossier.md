## Editorial frame and placement audit

This account treats *Borealosuchus* as an amphibious freshwater/coastal-plain crocodyliform. The roster type `marine_reptile` is misleading and must not drive copy. Shore placement beside a river or pond is sound; open-ocean placement is not. Species-level attribution is less secure for isolated teeth and osteoderms than for diagnostic skull material.

## Disputes and guardrails

- Do not call it a modern crocodile, alligator, or marine reptile.
- Do not assign every Hell Creek crocodyliform fragment to this species.
- Diet beyond general carnivory is inferred; no species-specific prey list is established.
- Do not claim *B. sternbergii* itself crossed the K–Pg boundary merely because congeners or related lineages did.
- Color, voice, sociality, and the depicted open-mouth behavior are artistic.
