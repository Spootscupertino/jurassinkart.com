## Limitations and guardrails

- Harrell and Martin support the South Dakota identity and 68.3–67.6 Ma age, **not** a 17 m length.
- Do not portray an eel-like animal, a dorsal fin, snake-style “unhinging,” social hunting, or known coloration.
- Global occurrences and isolated teeth require taxonomic caution.
- The exact tail outline is transferred from close relatives.
