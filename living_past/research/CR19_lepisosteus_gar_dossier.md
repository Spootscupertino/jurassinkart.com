# CR19 — *Lepisosteus* gar research dossier

**Version:** 1.0.0-pilot · **Reviewed:** 2026-07-29 · **External review:** None

## Identity and placement audit

Hell Creek gar fossils are frequently isolated scales and teeth. Family-level Lepisosteidae may be safer than *Lepisosteus* for many specimens, so the public identity is *Lepisosteus* sp. with an explicit caveat. Freshwater/backwater placement is strong; open marine placement is not. The 2 m size is an illustrative upper bound, not a measured local individual.

## Guardrails

Do not identify every scale to genus; do not borrow alligator gar size for *Lepisosteus*; do not state air-gulping was directly observed in fossils; avoid “unchanged living fossil.”
