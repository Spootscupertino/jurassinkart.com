## Disputes, limitations, and language guardrails

- Do not turn an ecosystem inference into a Hell Creek occurrence record.
- Do not identify a species, parent, diet, burrow-maker, or behavior more precisely than the cited fossils allow.
- Do not treat a living analogue as an unchanged survivor or direct descendant.
- Body color, soft tissues, pose, and moment-to-moment behavior are reconstruction choices unless explicitly listed as direct evidence.
- The poster enlarges small and microscopic subjects for visibility; its figure size is not a literal common scale with dinosaurs.
