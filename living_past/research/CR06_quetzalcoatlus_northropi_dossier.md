## Editorial frame

The taxon is temporally suitable but geographically mismatched: confirmed fossils are from Texas, not Hell Creek–Lance. The page retains it as a transparent regional extrapolation and distinguishes giant *Q. northropi* material from the smaller, more complete *Q. lawsoni* used in restorations.

## Disputes and guardrails

- Never label *Q. northropi* a Hell Creek animal.
- Keep “about 10 m” as an estimate, not a measured complete wingspan.
- Distinguish *Q. lawsoni* anatomy from giant *Q. northropi* evidence.
- Flight is secure; precise speed, range, and launch performance are model-dependent.
- Terrestrial foraging is favored but no fossil meal fixes its diet.
- Pycnofibre placement, color, crest tissue, and exact wing outline are reconstructed.
