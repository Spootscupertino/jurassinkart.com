## Disputes, limitations, and guardrails

- **“Mummy” gut contents:** do not present plant material associated with historic specimens as an unquestioned last meal; transport or contamination is possible.
- **Taxonomy:** distinguish latest Maastrichtian *E. annectens* from older *E. regalis*. Explain older names only when useful.
- **Soft tissue:** the comb is directly preserved, but its living color and possible sex/age variation are unknown.
- **Locomotion:** portray facultative two- and four-legged movement; do not lock the animal into one stance.
- **Herding:** gatherings and bonebeds do not prove permanent, coordinated herds for every population.
- **Voice:** do not import resonating-tube claims from hollow-crested hadrosaurs.
