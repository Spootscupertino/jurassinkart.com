## Disputes, limitations, and guardrails

- **Torosaurus:** present both published positions. Do not state synonymy or separation as settled.
- **Horn use:** lesions support physical contact, not a universal fighting style. Display, recognition, defense, and combat are not mutually exclusive.
- **Skin:** impressions support scales in sampled areas. They do not establish color or dorsal quills.
- **Hands/posture:** avoid elephant feet and sprawling limbs. Exact elbow angle and soft-tissue outline remain reconstructed.
- **Diet:** low browsing is a supported synthesis; no exact preferred plant menu is known.
- **Social life:** do not claim permanent herds without direct evidence.

## Verification gaps

- No fossil establishes color, sound, stable herd structure, or a single exclusive horn function.
- The exact life-stage relationship between *Torosaurus* and *Triceratops* remains contested.
- R07 should receive a bibliographic check before publication if the skin paper’s final journal metadata is used in display copy; the public text relies only on the conservative observation that skin impressions preserve scales.
