## Editorial frame

The Hell Creek occurrence is secure at ornithomimid family level, while assignment to the fragment-based Colorado species *O. velox* is provisional. Feather evidence comes directly from older Canadian *O. edmontonicus*. These boundaries appear throughout the public copy.
