## Editorial frame

This is a high-risk taxon audit, not a conventional celebratory profile. The name is retained to match the poster roster, while the copy makes clear that the type lot is chimeric and the familiar reconstruction is provisional. No quotations, media, or outreach are included.

## Disputes and guardrails

- Never call the skeleton complete, associated, or definitively five to six metres long.
- State that the locality fits perfectly but the specimen-level reconstruction does not.
- Do not treat the corrected turtle furcula as a minor typo; it documents a mixed lot.
- Feathering is reasonable for a dromaeosaur. Exact wing shape, body size, speed, prey, and sociality are not known.
- “Valid genus” remains the published name used here, not a claim that later reassessment is finished.
