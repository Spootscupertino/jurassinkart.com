# CR18 — *Basilemys* research dossier

**Version:** 1.0.0-pilot · **Reviewed:** 2026-07-29 · **External review:** None

## Placement and identity audit

Use genus-level *Basilemys* and do not silently upgrade it to a species. It was a robust, mainly terrestrial nanhsiungchelyid, not a marine turtle. A walking pose on firm shore/floodplain ground is supportable; swimming offshore is not. The original “herbivore” label is softened because diet is inferred rather than directly preserved.

## Guardrails

No direct stomach contents support a strict herbivore label. No marine flippers are known. Do not use another *Basilemys* species’ full reconstruction as direct evidence for every Hell Creek fragment. Color and behavior are artistic.
