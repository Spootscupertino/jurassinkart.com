# CR20 — *Myledaphus bipartitus* research dossier

**Version:** 1.0.0-pilot · **Reviewed:** 2026-07-29 · **External review:** None

## Placement and identity audit

Freshwater or estuarine placement is supported; open-ocean placement is not required. “Guitarfish” is retained as a public handle while the text uses broader “ray/batomorph” language because current skeletal research may refine its exact position. Size is a range, not a tooth-derived certainty.

## Guardrails

Do not identify exact prey, claim exclusively freshwater conditions at every occurrence, or copy a modern guitarfish body uncritically. The new 2026 skeletal paper should receive specialist review before publication.
