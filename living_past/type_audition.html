Proposed: all-caps, +.22em, Optima for the small brass furniture labels (stratum heads, panel titles, legend). Enough air to read as an engraved museum plate; not so wide it fragments at ~0.5 cqw print size. Numerals and callouts stay in Cinzel to tie back to the title.

Proposed lock

  • Display / wordmark: Cinzel (free OFL Trajan-alike; upgrade to Trajan Pro later if desired). Inscriptional Roman capitals = the literal “carved in a museum wall” voice, and it's the one face here derived from Roman monuments — dead-on for “Sections of Ancient Earth.” Didot/Bodoni read fashion-magazine elegant, a touch cold for deep time.
  • Body serif: EB Garamond — warm humanist old-style that sets the blurb and italic species names beautifully; also free/OFL and available identically on web + print. Hoefler stays a fine fallback.
  • Utility sans: Optima — keep it. It's a humanist sans itself drawn from Roman inscriptions, so it shares Cinzel's DNA: one family feel across three roles.
  • Type scale: the ladder in #3 above (7 fixed roles, 2 weights: Cinzel 600 + Optima/Garamond regular-italic).
  • Brass labels: all-caps Optima, +.22em.
  • Net system: Cinzel (display + numerals) · EB Garamond (body + names) · Optima (labels/stats). Two of the three are free/OFL and web-identical — clean for the Astro side.
