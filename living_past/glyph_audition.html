Notes

Seven brass silhouettes, single consistent weight, all sitting on the same visual baseline. Invertebrate uses the ammonite spiral — the most instantly-readable paleo-invertebrate mark; it covers ammonites, belemnites, crustaceans and insects as the catch-all (Plant separately covers flora; note that fungi will ride under a small “other/plant-kingdom” note or get its own 8th glyph if you want botanical purity).

If any glyph reads ambiguously at legend size, the usual fixes are: exaggerate the one defining feature (theropod's raised tail, pterosaur's wing crook, mosasaur's paddle) and drop interior detail. Say which to refine and I'll iterate.
